02 / Behind Exind

A place to connect the dots.
And keep asking questions.

Exind is my personal space for exploring interests that don’t fit neatly into one box. I’m drawn to the way engineering, medicine, and agriculture each approach a shared challenge: improving everyday life.

This is a starting point for ideas, learning, and conversations across those fields. The question at the center of it all: what becomes possible when we learn from each other?

03 / Field notes

Questions worth exploring.

01What makes a design useful in the real world?

A good starting point is the person who will use it. What do they need? What gets in their way? I’m interested in how observation, simple prototypes, and feedback can shape more practical engineering ideas.

02How can technology support more human care?

New tools raise questions beyond what is technically possible. Accessibility, clarity, and the experiences of patients and care teams are all directions I want to explore when thinking about technology in medicine.

03What can engineering learn from the natural world?

Agriculture invites us to think in connected systems: soil, water, plants, climate, and people. I’m curious about designs that work with those relationships and respond to the realities of a particular place.
